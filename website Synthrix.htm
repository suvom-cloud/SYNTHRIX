About us

SYNTHRIX Studios is an independent game development studio driven by creativity, passion, and innovation. Our mission is simple: to craft immersive worlds and unforgettable gaming experiences that inspire players across the globe.

Founded in 2025, SYNTHRIX brings together young, ambitious developers and storytellers who believe in pushing boundaries. From action-packed adventures to horror-filled journeys, we focus on games that combine storytelling, gameplay, and atmosphere in a unique way.
